# Brand and Promotion

Image Gen 2 · Photo Lab. The class app for the Yazaki Makerspace AI image class, after [Restore and Remix](https://im4creative.github.io/MS-PL-Restore-and-Remix/).

**Open the class:** https://im4creative.github.io/MS-PL-Brand-and-Promotion/

You roll a brand nobody asked for, sketch it on paper, then build it with Gemini one layer at a time: a 1-bit logo, a product, the product in use, a layout, and one finished ad. Every prompt runs Subject, Setting, Structure, Style.

- One file: `index.html`. No build step, no server.
- Works with the Gemini app (copy the prompt out, bring the image back) or Direct sync with your own Gemini API key. The key stays in your browser.
- Your work saves in this browser only. On a shared computer, use **Clear this computer for the next maker** in Your kit.
- Your kit exports a storybook: a short PowerPoint of your sketches, each kept image, the prompt you actually sent, and your final ad.
- Example images, full size, with the exact prompts:
  - [`examples/class-run/`](examples/class-run/): the Rise & Purl example, from sketch to final ad.
  - [`examples/vocab/`](examples/vocab/): the images behind the vocabulary cards.
  - [`examples/styles/`](examples/styles/): the same final ad in all six class styles. Only the Style clause changes.
- Every example image was made in Gemini, except the two pencil sketches and the layout in `class-run/`, which were drawn for the test run.

Life is what we make it.
