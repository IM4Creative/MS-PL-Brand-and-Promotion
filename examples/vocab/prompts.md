# Vocabulary thumbnails

Made in one Gemini chat for the vocabulary cards. Image 1 was `reference-jar-front.jpg`, the front view cut from the class run's turnaround. Each prompt after the first was a follow-up in the same chat.

Make a hero shot: one jar, centered, eye level, on a seamless warm light-gray paper backdrop. Soft studio lighting, a soft shadow under the jar. Wide 16:9 landscape frame with plenty of empty space on both sides. No other objects, no added text. |
