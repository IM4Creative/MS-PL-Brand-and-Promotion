# One clause, six ads

The Rise & Purl final ad, run in all six class styles. Each run was a new Gemini chat with the same three uploads from [`../class-run/`](../class-run/): `3-logo.jpg` (image 1), `5-setting.jpg` (image 2) and `6-layout.png` (image 3).

Every prompt is identical except the last line, the Style clause. That's the class rule: change one clause, see what it does.

```
Aspect ratio 4:5. A portrait poster ad for “Rise & Purl”.
Image 1 is my logo. Image 2 is my product in its setting. Image 3 is my layout.
Subject: The sourdough starter jar from image 2 and the logo from image 1, exactly as shown.
Setting: A knitter feeds it flour between rows in a sunny yarn shop today, crisp morning air.
Structure: Follow the layout in image 3. Use it for placement only. Don't draw its boxes or labels. Logo, small, in the top-left corner. Headline across the top, under the logo and the empty space. Product, large, across the bottom two-thirds, under the headline. Leave the top-right corner completely empty, with no text and no picture. Headline text: “Rise to the occasion”.
Style: …
```
